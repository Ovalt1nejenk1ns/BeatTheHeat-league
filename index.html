League rules

The quick version. Your box's rule sheet is the final word.

Playing a round

  1. 104 cards, numbered 1 to 104. Deal 10 to each player and start 4 rows with one face-up card each.
  2. Everyone picks a card face down at the same time, then reveals.
  3. Lowest card goes first. Each card goes at the end of the row whose last card is the closest lower number.
  4. 6th card in a row? You take the first 5 cards and their flames, and your card starts the row over.
  5. Too low for every row? Take any row you choose; your card replaces it.
  6. Play until everyone's hand is empty, then add up the flames each player took.

Scoring

  • Every card shows its heat in flames. The flames on the cards you take are your points for the round.
  • Points are bad. Keep a running total.
  • When someone reaches the limit (66 in Take 5 / 6 nimmt!, which Beat the Heat is based on), the game ends and the lowest total wins.
  • If your rule sheet uses a different limit, pick it at setup.
